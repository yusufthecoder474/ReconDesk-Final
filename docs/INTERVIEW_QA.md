# Interview Q&A
Q: What business problem? A: Invoice and bank-payment records are often maintained separately; reconciliation exceptions take manual effort.
Q: Why Django? A: Built-in authentication, ORM, forms, admin and DRF.
Q: Why MySQL? A: Relational constraints, transactional allocation and SQL aggregates.
Q: Why two APIs? A: Django owns data; Flask returns stateless suggestions. This is a teaching choice, not an optimal production architecture.
Q: What did you measure? A: Pure rule tests ran locally. End-to-end performance and business savings are not measured.
Q: Biggest limitation? A: No production deployment, no immutable financial ledger, no independent real-world evaluation.
